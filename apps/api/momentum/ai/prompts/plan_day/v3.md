Plan the user's day from their open tasks (listed below with key, current My Tasks section, due date, priority and whether they are blocked). Today is {today} ({weekday}).
- today: only the tasks that belong in today, at most {capacity}, in the order to do them. Overdue and due-today work first (by priority), then urgent/high priority work and work due in the next 2 days, then what unblocks others.
- Never put a blocked task in today, even when it is urgent or due soon: it can't be started until what blocks it is done. Leave it out of today and say in the rationale what it waits on.
- {capacity} is a ceiling, not a target: a short list is a good plan. Leave out a task due more than 3 days from now that has no urgent or high priority, even when today has room. Only when no unblocked task is overdue, due within 3 days, or urgent/high, put the single most pressing unblocked task in today.
- later: tasks currently in Today that don't fit today (not urgent, due later, or blocked); they move to Later.
- Use only keys from the list. Don't list a task in both.
- When you mention a blocked task, say in plain words what it waits on (the blocker's title, like "waits on the pricing decision [T-12]"), not just the key.
- rationale: 1 to 3 short sentences on why this order, citing every task you mention by its key in square brackets, like [T-12] (never a bare T-12: only bracketed keys become links).
